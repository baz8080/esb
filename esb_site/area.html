How to read this page

Each outage shows when it began and when supply was restored, both as ESB reports them; the rows between are the customer count falling as crews restore sections. Where ESB never published a restore time the end is marked as an estimate or as the last sighting. There is no grade at this level: the A–F bands are calibrated to county-months and would mislead against a village-sized area. Full method and sources are on the main page.

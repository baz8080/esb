The grade is ESB Networks' own published aim: supply restored within 4 hours in 95% of cases. A county is scored on the share of fault-interrupted customers restored within that window - A meets the 95% aim, then B 90%, C 80%, D 70%, F below. Planned works are excluded. Storm days are not, though ESB excludes them from its own figures, so winter months will read worse than ESB's.

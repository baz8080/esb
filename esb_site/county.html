The grade is ESB Networks' own published aim: supply restored in under 4 hours in 95% of cases. A county is scored on the share of fault-interrupted customers back inside that window — A meets the 95% aim, then B 90%, C 80%, D 70%, F below. Planned works are excluded.

How to read this page

Each outage shows when it began and when supply was restored, both as ESB reports them; the rows between are the customer count falling as crews restore sections. Where ESB never published a restore time the end is marked as an estimate or as the last sighting.

Storm days are not excluded here, though ESB excludes them from its own figures, so winter months will read worse than ESB's. Full method and sources are on the main page.
